# Melvor Ledger roadmap

What players would pay for, ordered by value versus effort. Done items stay listed so the scope is visible.

## Shipped
- Money makers: best overall / from scratch / from your bank / leave it running / value added
- Rates with shop upgrades, equipped tools and mastery guild rank rewards
- Mastery guild ranks read from the save
- Training planner: fastest route to any level, supplies, gold made or spent
- Upgrade advisor: gain, payback, time to afford
- Bank: process first, safe to sell, full contents
- Item lookup (Ctrl K), next unlocks, progress since last import, copy summary, level-99 explorer
- Hunting (survey, track, trap with trap costs) in money makers, training and guild tasks; Ranching as passive per-animal income
- Pets: bonuses from found pets, expected time to each pet, pet guild tasks; potion advisor (net gain after potion cost)
- Better money within reach: unlocks that beat your best rate, with levels and training time (Next unlocks + Money makers banner)
- Guild task planner: every task with live progress, time estimates, and the fastest set of tasks to the next rank (in Training)

## Up next, in order
1. **Combat & Slayer.** Enemies and areas as money makers: kill speed from your gear and levels, drops and gold per hour, slayer coins, and where to farm materials that only drop from combat. (Moved up at the player's request.)
2. **More bonus sources.** Agility obstacles and astrology nodes.
3. **Farming planner.** Per-plot profit per growth cycle with compost, the best crop for each plot type, and a timer-aware schedule.
4. **Completion tracker.** Items found versus all items, from the save's statistics, with where to get each missing one.
5. **Goals.** Pin goals ("Smithing 60", "buy Steel Anvil", "100k gold") and get one combined plan with time estimates, re-checked on every import.
6. **Progress history.** Charts of total level, XP/day, gold and bank value across imports. Pace estimates ("99 Fishing in ~23 days at your current pace").
7. **Item & recipe browser.** Filterable database of every item and recipe with values, sources and uses.
8. **Multiple characters.** Switch between saved characters and compare them.
9. **Shareable cards.** An image card of your profile and best methods for Discord.
