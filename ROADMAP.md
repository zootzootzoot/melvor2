# Melvor Ledger roadmap

What players would pay for, ordered by value versus effort. Done items stay listed so the scope is visible.

## Shipped
- Money makers: best overall / from scratch / from your bank / leave it running / value added
- Rates with shop upgrades, equipped tools and mastery guild rank rewards
- Mastery guild ranks read from the save
- Training planner: fastest route to any level, supplies, gold made or spent
- Upgrade advisor: gain, payback, time to afford
- Bank: process first, safe to sell, full contents
- Item lookup (Ctrl K), next unlocks, progress since last import, copy summary, level-99 explorer
- Guild task planner: every task with live progress, time estimates, and the fastest set of tasks to the next rank (in Training)

## Up next, in order
1. **Hunting & Ranching.** Model both skills (traps, bait, species, animals, enclosures) in money makers and training.
2. **More bonus sources.** Pets, agility obstacles, astrology nodes, and potions you could equip, so rates match the game's stat screen. Includes a "which potion pays for itself" advisor.
3. **Farming planner.** Per-plot profit per growth cycle with compost, the best crop for each plot type, and a timer-aware schedule.
4. **Combat & Slayer planner.** Enemies, drops, kill speed from your gear and levels, gold/hr and XP/hr per area, and slayer coin income.
5. **Completion tracker.** Items found versus all items, from the save's statistics, with where to get each missing one.
6. **Goals.** Pin goals ("Smithing 60", "buy Steel Anvil", "100k gold") and get one combined plan with time estimates, re-checked on every import.
7. **Progress history.** Charts of total level, XP/day, gold and bank value across imports. Pace estimates ("99 Fishing in ~23 days at your current pace").
8. **Item & recipe browser.** Filterable database of every item and recipe with values, sources and uses.
9. **Multiple characters.** Switch between saved characters and compare them.
10. **Shareable cards.** An image card of your profile and best methods for Discord.
