# Melvor Ledger roadmap

What players would pay for, ordered by value versus effort. Done items stay listed so the scope is visible.

## Shipped
- Slayer (current task, coins/XP/gold per master, Slayer training) and dungeons (wave-by-wave runs, end rewards, chests valued at contents)
- Goals on the Plan tab: route to a quest, level or unlock with resolved prerequisites and time-saving upgrade detours
- Collection tracker (Bank) and pace across imports (Plan)
- Plan tab: speedrun-style route that picks the fastest-paying next step and resolves its training, gold and quest prerequisites; income chart over play time
- Quests: parsed from the save (goal progress), quest requirements resolved, quest-only actions and bosses estimated, hand-in items costed, unlocks valued
- Money makers: best overall / from scratch / from your bank / leave it running / value added
- Rates with shop upgrades, equipped tools and mastery guild rank rewards
- Mastery guild ranks read from the save
- Training planner: fastest route to any level, supplies, gold made or spent
- Upgrade advisor: gain, payback, time to afford
- Bank: process first, safe to sell, full contents
- Item lookup (Ctrl K), next unlocks, progress since last import, copy summary, level-99 explorer
- Hunting (survey, track, trap with trap costs) in money makers, training and guild tasks; Ranching as passive per-animal income
- Pets: bonuses from found pets, expected time to each pet, pet guild tasks; potion advisor (net gain after potion cost)
- Better money within reach: unlocks that beat your best rate, with levels and training time (Next unlocks + Money makers banner)
- Combat money makers (v1): every enemy from your gear and levels, with hit chance, kill time, damage taken costed as food, drops and gold
- Combat gear advice: equippable gear from bank, crafting or shop that raises your best combat rate
- Training counts input gathering/crafting time and XP from intermediate steps; thieving and combat food costs
- Combat skill training: Attack/Strength/Defence/Hitpoints/Ranged routes with the best enemy and stance
- Combat across all styles: per enemy, the best melee/ranged/magic loadout from owned gear (triangle, elements, spells, ammo and rune costs); XP training by matching style and stance; gear advice per enemy
- Guild task planner: every task with live progress, time estimates, and the fastest set of tasks to the next rank (in Training)

## Up next, in order
1. **Combat, part 3.** Enemy special attacks and passives, prayers, kill-count unlocks read from the save.
2. **More bonus sources.** Agility obstacles and astrology nodes.
3. **Farming planner.** Per-plot profit per growth cycle with compost, the best crop for each plot type, and a timer-aware schedule.
4. **Item & recipe browser.** Filterable database of every item and recipe with values, sources and uses.
5. **Multiple characters.** Switch between saved characters and compare them.
6. **Shareable cards.** An image card of your profile and best methods for Discord.
